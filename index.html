Who we are

Not a club.
A movement.

AIESEC is a global platform for young people to develop their leadership potential — youth-led, non-political and not-for-profit, powered by exchange. In Zagazig, we turn students into leaders who move the world.

  • 1948Founded after WWII to build peace through youth
  • 100+Countries & territories, one network
  • 40K+Young members leading worldwide
  • 1M+Alumni who started exactly here

Our DNA

Six values.
One direction.

  • 01

    Activating Leadership

    We lead by example — and spark leadership in everyone we work with.

  • 02

    Demonstrating Integrity

    We do what is right, not what is easy — and our actions match our words.

  • 03

    Living Diversity

    Every background is a strength. We seek difference and celebrate it.

  • 04

    Enjoying Participation

    Youth energy is the engine. We show up loud, proud and joyful.

  • 05

    Striving for Excellence

    Good enough never is. We raise the bar — then raise it again.

  • 06

    Acting Sustainably

    We build for the next generation — not just the next event.
