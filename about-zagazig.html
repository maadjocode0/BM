Where Zagazig's youth become leaders.

AIESEC in Zagazig is a youth-led, non-political, not-for-profit platform that develops leadership through cross-cultural exchange — connecting our students to the world, and bringing the world to Sharqia.

Who we are

A movement, rooted locally.

We turn students at Zagazig University into leaders who move the world — one exchange, one project, one team at a time.

As part of AIESEC — present in over 100 countries and territories since 1948 — our chapter gives young people something a lecture hall can't: real responsibility, an international network, and a reason to grow. We run exchange programs that send students abroad and host volunteers from around the world right here in Zagazig.

The result is leadership you can feel — in the classroom, in the community, and in every young person who discovers what they're capable of.
