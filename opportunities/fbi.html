FBI — Fingerprint

Music & Art · Zagazig, Egypt 🇪🇬

Bring music and art to children at a local academy — and leave your fingerprint on a young generation.

Zagazig, Sharqia — EgyptLocation
6 weeksDuration
≈30 hrs / week · weekends freeWorking hours
Egypt 🇪🇬Country

01Opportunity overview

FBI is part of AIESEC in Zagazig's Fingerprint project. You'll run creative music and art sessions for children, using the universal language of art to build their confidence, imagination and joy across six unforgettable weeks.

02About the organization

You'll work with a children's learning academy in Zagazig, hosted and supported end-to-end by AIESEC in Zagazig's award-winning experience team.

AIESEC in Zagazig — the #1 local committee in Egypt for incoming Global Volunteer (term 25.26). Our Customer Experience team holds the country's highest approved-to-completed conversion rate, so you're supported from the first message to the airport goodbye.

03Responsibilities

  • Plan and deliver music and art activities for children
  • Create a fun, safe and inclusive classroom
  • Encourage creativity, teamwork and self-expression
  • Prepare simple materials and small performances
  • Share your own culture through art

04Requirements

  • A background or genuine passion in music or art
  • Conversational English (B1 or above)
  • Comfortable working with children
  • Female applicants preferred for this project
  • Open-minded, patient and reliable

05Benefits

  • Covered accommodation for the whole stay
  • Airport pickup on arrival
  • A dedicated EP buddy
  • Global certificate on completion
  • Outings and a Global Village celebration

06Accommodation, food & transport

Accommodation

Comfortable accommodation is arranged and covered by the hosting team for the full duration of the project.

Food

Some meals are provided; food in Zagazig is affordable and delicious, with vegetarian options widely available.

Transportation

Airport pickup on arrival, plus support getting around the city and to the project each day.
