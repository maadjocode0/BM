The programme

What is Global Volunteer?

A cross-cultural exchange

A volunteering experience abroad, run by AIESEC — the world's largest youth-led organization, present in 100+ countries. You live, work and grow in a brand-new culture.

Rooted in the SDGs

Every project tackles a real challenge tied to a UN Sustainable Development Goal — from quality education to good health, decent work and life below water.

Leadership, earned

It's not a holiday. You take real responsibility, work in an international team and come home with a leadership story — and a global certificate to prove it.

The journey

From apply to takeoff.

1

Apply

Pick a project and apply on the official AIESEC platform. It takes five minutes — no CV of a lifetime required.

2

Get matched

A quick interview with our team, then we match you to the project and dates that fit you best.

3

Prepare

Visa support, accommodation and logistics — sorted together with your dedicated EP buddy.

4

Travel

Land in Egypt to an airport pickup and settle into your covered accommodation in Zagazig.

5

Volunteer

Four to eight weeks of real impact on your SDG project — with outings, trips and a Global Village along the way.

6

Return

Head home with a global certificate, a second family and a story that genuinely travels.

Can you join?

Who can apply.

Young people aged 18–30

Students and recent graduates from anywhere in the world are welcome.

Conversational English (B1+)

A few projects also welcome German or French speakers. No Arabic needed.

An open mind > a perfect CV

Most projects need zero prior experience — just curiosity and commitment.

A background for some tracks

Heartbeat needs a medical or science background; Fingerprint looks for music or art.

A valid passport

That's the one document you bring — we support you with the visa.

The essentials

  • Duration: 4–8 weeks, flexible start dates
  • Hosting fee: $75–$90 (TN fee)
  • On The Map packages: from $270 (4 wks) / $395 (6 wks)
  • Languages: English B1+, some German / French
  • Included: stay, pickup, buddy, certificate
  • Campuses: Zagazig, Obour, Benha, 10th of Ramadan
